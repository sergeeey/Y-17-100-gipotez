`[OPEN]` **Bound `|Δ_iΔ_jX|` (the discrete second-order difference of the LOG-VALUE `X=log(θ/√n)`) via dual-slack/margin/near-tie structure of the active LP constraints, not via the norm of the difference between two optimal dual vectors.**
