### (c) Lemma D and regular outcomes

**CONFIRMED.**

The proof in [THEOREM_S_PLUS_EXISTENCE_v6.md, lines 49–51](<E:/Проверка Гипотез/работаю над проверкой гипотез/Y-17 100 gipotez/experiments/20261028-h-cat56-2-phase4/stage_e_v1/THEOREM_S_PLUS_EXISTENCE_v6.md:49>) is sound:

1. \(M=CC^T\), with \(C\) invertible.
2. \(g=C^Te_N\ne0\).
3. A Householder orthogonal matrix sends \(g/\|g\|\) to \(h=(1,\ldots,1)/\sqrt N\).
4. Hence every rotated column \(c'_k\) has the same last coordinate
   \[
   s=\|g\|/\sqrt N>0.
   \]
5. Dividing every \(c'_k\) by \(s\) gives \((\hat c_k)_N=1\), while placing \(s^2\) in the weight gives
   \[
   \omega=s^2=\frac{\|g\|^2}{N}=\frac{M_{NN}}N>0.
   \]

A raw Cholesky column having last coordinate different from 1 is therefore harmless. The column rotation makes the final last coordinates equal to the same positive \(s\); normalization makes them exactly 1. More generally, a nonzero real last coordinate could be absorbed into the weight, but a zero last coordinate would create an irregular support-free outcome. Lemma D specifically prevents that case.

The exact membership audit checks each stored basis vector against

\[
A_i^\dagger y=\lambda_i x,\qquad \lambda_i\in\mathbb R
\]

using an independently coded cross-product and reality test at [audit_ys_exact.py, lines 73–103](<E:/Проверка Гипотез/работаю над проверкой гипотез/Y-17 100 gipotez/experiments/20261028-h-cat56-2-phase4/stage_e_v1/code/audit_ys_exact.py:73>). It records 1,484 basis vectors, zero failures at [audit_ys_exact.json, lines 3–8](<E:/Проверка Гипотез/работаю над проверкой гипотез/Y-17 100 gipotez/experiments/20261028-h-cat56-2-phase4/stage_e_v1/logs/audit_ys_exact.json:3>).

### (e) YPZJ regular saturation and the full matrix

**CONFIRMED as an application; external theorem source UNCHECKED.**

The theorem artifact quotes the regular-outcome condition

\[
\Pi L_i\rho=\xi_i\Pi\rho,\qquad \xi_i\in\mathbb R
\]

at [lines 22–26](<E:/Проверка Гипотез/работаю над проверкой гипотез/Y-17 100 gipotez/experiments/20261028-h-cat56-2-phase4/stage_e_v1/THEOREM_S_PLUS_EXISTENCE_v6.md:22>).

For \(\Pi=d\,uu^\dagger\), \(u=(\alpha;y)\), the reduction is correct:

\[
u^\dagger L_i\rho=\xi_i u^\dagger\rho
\iff y^\dagger A_iQ=\xi_i\alpha^\dagger Q
\iff A_i^\dagger y=\xi_i\alpha,
\]

because \(Q\succ0\) is invertible and \(\xi_i\) is real. This is stated at [lines 25–28](<E:/Проверка Гипотез/работаю над проверкой гипотез/Y-17 100 gipotez/experiments/20261028-h-cat56-2-phase4/stage_e_v1/THEOREM_S_PLUS_EXISTENCE_v6.md:25>).

With \(\alpha=x_j\) and \(y\) a real linear combination of the stored \(B_{j,a}\), exact \(Y_S\)-membership supplies precisely that condition.

It also gives equality as a full matrix, not merely equality of traces. For one outcome:

\[
\partial_i p_k=\xi_{k,i}p_k,
\]

so its CFI contribution is

\[
\frac{\partial_i p_k\,\partial_j p_k}{p_k}
=p_k\xi_{k,i}\xi_{k,j}.
\]

The same saturation condition makes the corresponding quantum-bound contribution equal to that expression for every pair \(i,j\). Summing over outcomes and using completeness gives

\[
\sum_k\Re\operatorname{Tr}(\rho L_iE_kL_j)
=\Re\operatorname{Tr}(\rho L_iL_j)
=F^Q_{ij}.
\]

This is the argument at [theorem lines 57–64](<E:/Проверка Гипотез/работаю над проверкой гипотез/Y-17 100 gipotez/experiments/20261028-h-cat56-2-phase4/stage_e_v1/THEOREM_S_PLUS_EXISTENCE_v6.md:57>).

[UNCHECKED] I did not independently inspect arXiv:1806.07337; it was not included among the primary artifacts. Thus I confirm the application of the theorem as quoted, not the external citation’s exact wording or hypotheses.

### (c) Lemma D and regular outcomes

**CONFIRMED.**

The proof in [THEOREM_S_PLUS_EXISTENCE_v6.md, lines 49–51](<E:/Проверка Гипотез/работаю над проверкой гипотез/Y-17 100 gipotez/experiments/20261028-h-cat56-2-phase4/stage_e_v1/THEOREM_S_PLUS_EXISTENCE_v6.md:49>) is sound:

1. \(M=CC^T\), with \(C\) invertible.
2. \(g=C^Te_N\ne0\).
3. A Householder orthogonal matrix sends \(g/\|g\|\) to \(h=(1,\ldots,1)/\sqrt N\).
4. Hence every rotated column \(c'_k\) has the same last coordinate
   \[
   s=\|g\|/\sqrt N>0.
   \]
5. Dividing every \(c'_k\) by \(s\) gives \((\hat c_k)_N=1\), while placing \(s^2\) in the weight gives
   \[
   \omega=s^2=\frac{\|g\|^2}{N}=\frac{M_{NN}}N>0.
   \]

A raw Cholesky column having last coordinate different from 1 is therefore harmless. The column rotation makes the final last coordinates equal to the same positive \(s\); normalization makes them exactly 1. More generally, a nonzero real last coordinate could be absorbed into the weight, but a zero last coordinate would create an irregular support-free outcome. Lemma D specifically prevents that case.

The exact membership audit checks each stored basis vector against

\[
A_i^\dagger y=\lambda_i x,\qquad \lambda_i\in\mathbb R
\]

using an independently coded cross-product and reality test at [audit_ys_exact.py, lines 73–103](<E:/Проверка Гипотез/работаю над проверкой гипотез/Y-17 100 gipotez/experiments/20261028-h-cat56-2-phase4/stage_e_v1/code/audit_ys_exact.py:73>). It records 1,484 basis vectors, zero failures at [audit_ys_exact.json, lines 3–8](<E:/Проверка Гипотез/работаю над проверкой гипотез/Y-17 100 gipotez/experiments/20261028-h-cat56-2-phase4/stage_e_v1/logs/audit_ys_exact.json:3>).

\[
\operatorname{Tr}(\rho E)

### (e) YPZJ regular saturation and the full matrix

**CONFIRMED as an application; external theorem source UNCHECKED.**

The theorem artifact quotes the regular-outcome condition

\[
\Pi L_i\rho=\xi_i\Pi\rho,\qquad \xi_i\in\mathbb R
\]

at [lines 22–26](<E:/Проверка Гипотез/работаю над проверкой гипотез/Y-17 100 gipotez/experiments/20261028-h-cat56-2-phase4/stage_e_v1/THEOREM_S_PLUS_EXISTENCE_v6.md:22>).

For \(\Pi=d\,uu^\dagger\), \(u=(\alpha;y)\), the reduction is correct:

\[
u^\dagger L_i\rho=\xi_i u^\dagger\rho
\iff y^\dagger A_iQ=\xi_i\alpha^\dagger Q
\iff A_i^\dagger y=\xi_i\alpha,
\]

because \(Q\succ0\) is invertible and \(\xi_i\) is real. This is stated at [lines 25–28](<E:/Проверка Гипотез/работаю над проверкой гипотез/Y-17 100 gipotez/experiments/20261028-h-cat56-2-phase4/stage_e_v1/THEOREM_S_PLUS_EXISTENCE_v6.md:25>).

With \(\alpha=x_j\) and \(y\) a real linear combination of the stored \(B_{j,a}\), exact \(Y_S\)-membership supplies precisely that condition.

It also gives equality as a full matrix, not merely equality of traces. For one outcome:

\[
\partial_i p_k=\xi_{k,i}p_k,
\]

so its CFI contribution is

\[
\frac{\partial_i p_k\,\partial_j p_k}{p_k}
=p_k\xi_{k,i}\xi_{k,j}.
\]

The same saturation condition makes the corresponding quantum-bound contribution equal to that expression for every pair \(i,j\). Summing over outcomes and using completeness gives

\[
\sum_k\Re\operatorname{Tr}(\rho L_iE_kL_j)
=\Re\operatorname{Tr}(\rho L_iL_j)
=F^Q_{ij}.
\]

This is the argument at [theorem lines 57–64](<E:/Проверка Гипотез/работаю над проверкой гипотез/Y-17 100 gipotez/experiments/20261028-h-cat56-2-phase4/stage_e_v1/THEOREM_S_PLUS_EXISTENCE_v6.md:57>).

[UNCHECKED] I did not independently inspect arXiv:1806.07337; it was not included among the primary artifacts. Thus I confirm the application of the theorem as quoted, not the external citation’s exact wording or hypotheses.

## Most likely error and final assessment

**Single most likely error:** the certificate overstates the relationship between the two exact runs by saying they share \(y\) and implying that the same exact \(M^*\) was reverified twice. The code shows that the rational run computes \(y\), while the integer run loads a later checkpoint \(y\); their equality is not established. Conversely, the genuinely shared floating starting moments `exact_sdp2_M.pkl` are omitted from the shared-input list.

This does **not** refute existence: the integer path independently verifies all 484 equations and positivity for the candidate it reconstructs, and the rational path records the same properties for its candidate. It does weaken the narrower claim “two checks of one identical stored rational certificate.”

**Shared-input dependence:** primarily a **documentation/provenance gap, not a proof gap**, for existence on the stored exact grid. Candidate-generation inputs may be shared without invalidating an exact property verifier. It becomes a proof-scope issue only if the claim is strengthened from “existence for these stored exact \(x_j,B_{j,a}\)” to “independently reproduced from the raw discovery source and original physical instance.”
